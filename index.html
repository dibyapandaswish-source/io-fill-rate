At a glance

  
Fill rate is Sent ÷ Ordered, identical to the daily log. Short units is the gross shortfall felt at pod level — every pod's own shortfall added up, so an over-send to one pod never hides a starved pod elsewhere.

Was the cut shared, or did some pods get starved?

  
Each short item is classified by how the available quantity was split across the pods that ordered it. Pro-rata cut — everyone took a similar percentage hit (a production/supply problem). Uneven — some pods served in full while others got zero or near-zero (an allocation problem). Total stock-out — nothing sent to anyone.

Ordered → Sent → Received

  
Limited to internal orders that reached a terminal status (closed / received / force_closed) so the received leg is comparable — orders still confirmed or sent simply have not been booked in yet and would read as a false loss. Sent → Received is quantity that left the warehouse but was never GRN'd at the pod.

  





Source: daily internal order exports. Rows with Internal Order Status = ordered excluded. BLR Production In-house / F&V read the report date except the 7 night-dispatch CK RMs (previous day); Delhi-NCR Dry RM reads the previous day except the 4 GGN bakery items (report date); all other categories read the previous day.
